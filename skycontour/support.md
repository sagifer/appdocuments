# SkyContour – Support

SkyContour records your local horizon with the phone camera and exports it as a N.I.N.A. horizon file (.hrz) and a Stellarium landscape (.zip).

## Getting help

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at
[github.com/sagifer/appdocuments/issues](https://github.com/sagifer/appdocuments/issues).
Please mention your phone model, the iOS or Android version and the app version.

## Tips for a good survey

- **Calibrate the field of view once** (Settings › Calibrate with the camera): aim at distant detail and turn the phone slowly sideways. Without it, an average value is assumed.
- **Keep away from metal.** Cars, railings and metal tripod heads disturb the compass; if the compass looks unsure, move the phone in a figure-eight.
- **Follow the dot.** Turn until the dot sits in the circle and hold still; with Auto on, frames are taken by themselves. If trees or roofs reach the top of the frame, add a higher shot.
- **Survey in daylight or at dusk.** A bright sky against dark ground gives the cleanest edge. On the Review screen, fix the edge with *Paint sky* / *Paint ground*.
- **Check north.** Magnetic declination is not applied yet: if the profile looks rotated, correct it with *North offset* on the Review screen. It applies to both the .hrz and the panorama.

## Using the files

- **N.I.N.A.:** *Options › General › Astrometry › Custom Horizon*, select the exported `.hrz` file.
- **Stellarium:** *Sky and viewing options (F4) › Landscape › Add/remove landscapes › Install a new landscape from .zip*, then select the exported zip.
